About us

A neighbourhood salon with a premium standard of care

New Looks Beauty Salon is a ladies beauty salon based in Babhnauli, Siwan. We offer professional beauty services across makeup, hair, facial and skin care, waxing, threading, manicure and pedicure, nail services, mehendi and complete bridal packages.

Every appointment is taken one client at a time, in a clean and welcoming salon environment, with a focus on hygiene, comfort and results that suit you.

  • Makeup, hair, skin, nail, mehendi & bridal services
  • Bridal and occasion-ready appointments
  • A clean, welcoming ladies-only salon environment

The bridal experience

Bridal preparation, handled calmly from start to finish

Bridal makeup, hairstyling, facial, manicure, pedicure, waxing, threading and saree or dupatta draping — planned together so your wedding day runs smoothly.
